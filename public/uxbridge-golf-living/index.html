BEFORE YOU MAKE YOUR MOVE

A few good questions.

How should I compare homes around Uxbridge golf courses?

Start with your budget, home type and everyday needs. Then compare each property’s actual route to your preferred course, work and errands. A Uxbridge address alone does not tell you the drive time or whether a home is beside a course.

Does a nearby home include golf membership or course access?

Do not assume it does. Membership, tee times and access are separate matters to confirm with the club. This shortlist does not promise membership, course frontage or any club affiliation.

What will my personalized shortlist include?

We’ll consider available homes against your preferences and discuss course locations, practical routes and the trade-offs that matter to you. Availability changes, and we may need to refine your criteria together.

Can I request help if I am only exploring?

Yes. Choose “Just exploring” and tell us what interests you. Matthew will follow up personally; there is no obligation to move or to join a marketing list.
