Welcome to **NorthSide GTA Insights**, our newest series that dives deep into the trends shaping life north of the 407.

![Cyclist on a frosty morning trail](/uploads/insights/greenway-cyclist.jpg "Local commuters are already using the greenway as a car-free shortcut.")

The Aurora Greenway Revival is more than a park refresh. It's a multi-year investment that connects nine kilometres of protected pathways and links local schools, commuter lots, and future housing sites. Here are three quick takeaways:

1. **Commute Relief** — Aurora GO riders can now reach the station via a protected path system that avoids Yonge Street congestion.
2. **Neighbourhood Pride** — The plan adds wayfinding, lighting, and winter maintenance, making the network usable year-round.
3. **Future-Proof Growth** — Early planning documents show mixed-use zoning around gateway nodes, a sign of smart density.

> “It’s the kind of infrastructure move that keeps families in the NorthSide GTA instead of pulling them back to the core,” explains Landon Mulhall.

### What this means for buyers

If you're upgrading into Aurora, expect micro-neighbourhoods along the greenway to see renewed demand. Detached homes on the Woodhaven, Old Yonge, and Bayview edges are already drawing more showings per week.

### Sellers, take note

Listing along the new pathway? Lean into the lifestyle story. Highlight the five-minute bike ride to coffee on Yonge, the weekend farmers' market link, and the fresh lighting that extends evening use.
