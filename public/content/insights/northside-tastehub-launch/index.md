# Launching NorthSide TasteHub: A Community Food Vote for the NorthSide GTA

If you live in any of the towns we think of as the <span style="color:#32610E;">NorthSide GTA</span> — places like [Uxbridge](/uxbridge), [Georgina](/georgina), [East Gwillimbury](/east-gwillimbury), [Newmarket](/newmarket), [Aurora](/aurora), [Stouffville](/stouffville), or [Scugog](/scugog) — you already know this:

**Where you eat is a big part of why you love your town.**

It’s not just about having “somewhere” to grab food. It’s about:

- the pizza place your family swears by  
- the wings spot after a game  
- the date-night restaurant you recommend to everyone  
- the bakery you hit on Saturday mornings  

Spend a bit of time in any of the local Facebook groups — Uxbridge, Newmarket, Aurora, Keswick/Georgina, Stouffville, Port Perry and surrounding areas — and you’ll see the same questions come up again and again:

- “Who actually has the best pizza in town?”
- “Where’s your go-to spot for wings?”
- “Any suggestions for a good date-night restaurant?”
- “What’s a good place to take the family?”

These posts get dozens (and sometimes hundreds) of comments.  
Everyone has a favourite.  
Nobody agrees.  
And honestly… we love that.

We’re Matthew and Landon — brothers, local real estate agents, and the team behind <span style="color:#32610E;">Finally Home Agents</span> and <span style="color:#32610E;">NorthSide GTA</span>.  
We live here. We work here. We’re raising our families here. And just like you…

**We argue with our friends and family about where to eat.**

After years of watching these debates in local groups (and jumping into them ourselves), we decided it was time to build something the NorthSide GTA didn’t have:

**A community-powered hub where locals can actually vote on their favourite spots — and discover new ones.**

That’s how <span style="color:#32610E;">NorthSide TasteHub</span> was born.

---

## 🍕 Why We Built NorthSide TasteHub (And Why It Matters Here)

We work in real estate, but our day-to-day life is much bigger than that.

We’re out in these towns every day:

- grabbing a coffee between showings  
- meeting clients at local bakeries  
- taking our families out for pizza  
- running our Thursday Night Golf League across the NorthSide GTA  
- and constantly talking with people about where they love to eat  

Over and over, the same themes kept popping up:

### **1. Food is a huge part of small-town life.**
When people think about moving from downtown Toronto or a bigger suburb to one of our towns, this question always comes up:

**“Okay… but how’s the food here?”**

### **2. Good information is scattered.**
In a big city, you have endless:

- blogs  
- TikToks  
- listicles  

In smaller towns, the best information lives inside:

- local Facebook groups  
- Google reviews  
- word-of-mouth  
- the occasional article written by someone who may not even live here  

### **3. Locals wanted one simple place to check.**
We kept seeing the same comments:

- “Someone needs to write all these answers down.”  
- “We need a place with all of this in one spot.”  
- “We need a local list that isn’t just ads.”  

So we didn’t want a generic “Top 10” list.  
We wanted something that felt like the **town talking to itself**.

That’s the idea behind <span style="color:#32610E;">NorthSide TasteHub</span>.

---

## ⭐ What Exactly *Is* NorthSide TasteHub?

<span style="color:#32610E;">NorthSide TasteHub</span> is a section of [NorthSideGTA.ca](/) where locals can:

- **Vote** in live food polls  
- **See** which restaurants are leading  
- **Discover** new spots  
- **Suggest** places we should add  

It’s a **community scoreboard for food across the NorthSide GTA** — powered by locals, not ads.

It’s built for the same towns we help buyers and sellers with every day:

- **Uxbridge** – home to some of the fiercest pizza debates  
- **Georgina** – lakeside patios, takeout favourites, and strong community loyalty  
- **East Gwillimbury** – a fast-growing area with new restaurants popping up  
- **Newmarket** – a great mix around Main Street and beyond  
- **Aurora** – cozy pubs, date spots, and great takeout  
- **Stouffville** – a growing GO-connected town with legit local food  
- **Scugog** – especially Port Perry, full of charm and small-town favourites  

When you land on [NorthSide TasteHub](/tastehub), polls are organized by **town** and **category**, so everything stays local and relevant.

## 🗳 How TasteHub Works (It’s Super Simple)

We wanted TasteHub to be something you can use while standing in your kitchen saying:

**“Okay… where should we go?”**

Here’s how it works:

### **1. Pick a town + category**
Examples:

- “Uxbridge – Best Pizza”  
- “Newmarket – Best Wings”  
- “Aurora – Best Date-Night Spot”  
- “Georgina – Best Family-Friendly Restaurant”

### **2. Browse a curated list**
Every poll only includes spots **actually in that town**.

### **3. Cast your vote**
One click. No account needed.

### **4. Watch the rankings move**
Live, real-time updates.

### **5. Tell us what’s missing**
Locals can suggest restaurants for future polls.

That’s it.  
Fast. Free. Fun. Useful.

TasteHub was built from the same conversations we all have:

- Uxbridge → “Who actually has the best pizza?”  
- Newmarket → “Where are the best wings after a game?”  
- Aurora → “Best date-night spot that isn’t a chain?”  
- Georgina → “Best family-friendly restaurant?”  
- Stouffville + Scugog → “What’s the best small-town go-to?”  

In local Facebook groups, the pattern repeats:

Someone asks.  
Dozens of comments roll in.  
Two or three favourites rise.  
Hidden gems surface.  
Someone asks again a week later.

Even in our own families and friend groups, we’ve had full debates about:

- best pizza in Uxbridge  
- wings in Newmarket  
- underrated spots in Georgina  

TasteHub is simply a way to capture that energy — and put it somewhere useful.

## 🚫 What TasteHub Is *Not*

It’s important to be clear:

**NorthSide TasteHub is not:**

- a paid ranking system  
- a sponsored list  
- influenced by ads  
- written by people outside our area  
- a replacement for your personal favourites  

Rankings are powered by **real local votes**.  
If your community loves a place, it will show.
