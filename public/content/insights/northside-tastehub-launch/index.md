# Launching NorthSide TasteHub: A Community Food Vote for the NorthSide GTA

If you live in one of the towns we think of as the **NorthSide GTA** — places like
[Uxbridge](/uxbridge), [Georgina](/georgina), [East Gwillimbury](/east-gwillimbury),
[Newmarket](/newmarket), [Aurora](/aurora), [Stouffville](/stouffville) or
[Scugog](/scugog) — you already know this:

Where you eat is a big part of why you love your town.

It’s not just about having “somewhere” to grab food. It’s about:
- the pizza place your family swears by,  
- the wings spot after a game,  
- the date-night restaurant you recommend to everyone,  
- the bakery you hit on Saturday mornings.

Spend a bit of time in any of the local Facebook groups — Uxbridge, Newmarket, Aurora,
Keswick/Georgina, Stouffville, Port Perry and surrounding areas — and you’ll see the same
questions come up again and again:

- “Who actually has the best pizza in town?”  
- “Where’s your go-to spot for wings?”  
- “Any suggestions for a good date-night restaurant around here?”  
- “What’s a good place to take the family that isn’t a chain?”  

The posts get dozens (and sometimes hundreds) of comments. Everyone has a favourite.
Nobody agrees. And that’s kind of the fun of it.

We’re Matthew and Landon — brothers, local real estate agents, and the team behind
**Finally Home Agents** and **NorthSide GTA**. We live here, we work here, we’re raising our
families here, and we spend a lot of time doing the same thing you do:

Arguing with our friends and family about where to eat.

After years of watching these debates in local groups (and jumping into them ourselves),
we decided it was time to build something the NorthSide GTA didn’t have:

**A community-powered hub where locals can actually vote on their favourite spots — and
discover new ones.**

That’s how **NorthSide TasteHub** was born.

---

## Why we built NorthSide TasteHub

We work in real estate, but our day-to-day life is much bigger than that.

We’re out in these towns every day:
- grabbing a coffee between showings,  
- meeting clients at local bakeries,  
- taking our families out for pizza,  
- running our Thursday night golf league that moves around the NorthSide GTA,  
- and constantly talking to people about where they like to eat.

A few things kept coming up again and again:

1. **Food is a huge part of small-town life.**  
   When someone talks about moving from downtown Toronto or a bigger suburb to one of our
   towns, food is always near the top of the list of questions:  
   *“Okay, but how’s the food here?”*

2. **Good information is scattered.**  
   In a big city, you have endless blogs, TikToks and “Top 10” lists. In smaller towns,
   information lives in a mix of:
   - local Facebook groups,  
   - Google reviews,  
   - word-of-mouth,  
   - and the occasional article that may or may not be written by someone who actually
     lives here.

3. **Locals wanted one simple place to check.**  
   The more we watched those Facebook debates, the more we noticed the same comments:
   “Someone needs to write all these answers down,”  
   “I wish there was one site with all of this,”  
   “We really need a local list that isn’t just ads.”

We didn’t want another generic list or a “Best of” ranking written by people who passed
through once.

We wanted something that felt like the town talking to itself — and anyone who’s curious
enough to listen.

That’s the whole idea behind **NorthSide TasteHub**.

---

## What is NorthSide TasteHub?

**NorthSide TasteHub** is a section of [NorthSideGTA.ca](/) where locals can:

- **Vote** in live food polls across our towns  
- **See** which restaurants are leading in different categories  
- **Discover** new places they might not have tried yet  
- **Suggest** spots we should add next

Think of it as a **community scoreboard for food in the NorthSide GTA** — powered by
local votes, not by ads.

It’s built for the same towns we help buyers and sellers with every day:

- **Uxbridge** – where some of the most heated pizza and bakery debates live  
- **Georgina** – from Keswick to Pefferlaw, with lakeside patios and classic takeout spots  
- **East Gwillimbury** – growing quickly, with new options popping up all the time  
- **Newmarket** – a strong restaurant lineup around Main Street and beyond  
- **Aurora** – cozy pubs, date-night restaurants, and great takeout  
- **Stouffville** – an easy GO ride away, with its own solid food scene  
- **Scugog** – including Port Perry, where waterfront restaurants and small-town favourites shine

When you land on [NorthSide TasteHub](/tastehub), you’ll see polls organized by **town**
and by **category**, so the results stay local and relevant.

---

## How NorthSide TasteHub works

We wanted TasteHub to be simple enough that you can use it while you’re standing in your
kitchen saying, “Okay, so where should we go?”

Here’s how it works:

1. **Pick a town and category**  
   Choose something like:
   - “Uxbridge – Best Pizza”  
   - “Newmarket – Best Wings”  
   - “Aurora – Best Date-Night Spot”  
   - “Georgina – Best Family-Friendly Restaurant”

2. **Browse a curated list of local spots**  
   Each poll focuses on restaurants actually located in that town. No “kind of nearby”
   spots from somewhere else just to pad the list.

3. **Cast your vote**  
   Tap or click your pick. That’s it. No account required.

4. **Watch the rankings move**  
   As more people vote, you’ll see which spots are climbing the leaderboard and which
   places are quietly earning a loyal following.

5. **Tell us what’s missing**  
   If we missed a place that truly belongs in the conversation, there’s space on the page
   to let us know so we can add it to future polls.

It’s free, fast, and meant to be fun — and useful — for locals.

## The debates we see all the time

Before we wrote a single line of code, TasteHub really started with the debates we kept
seeing and hearing:

- Uxbridge: “Who actually has the best pizza?”  
- Newmarket: “Where do you go for wings after a game?”  
- Aurora: “Is there a go-to place for date night that isn’t a chain?”  
- Georgina: “Best family-friendly restaurant with good food?”  
- Stouffville & Scugog: “Where’s the best local spot that feels like ‘our place’?”

In the local Facebook groups, the pattern is always the same:

Someone asks a simple question.  
Dozens of comments flood in.  
Two or three places get mentioned over and over.  
A few hidden gems surface.  
And then someone else posts the same question a few days later.

Even within our own family and friend circles, we’ve had full debates about:
- who has the best pizza in Uxbridge,  
- where to go for wings in Newmarket,  
- or which takeaway spot in Georgina is the most underrated.

Everyone has their go-to places. Everyone has opinions. Nobody is shy about sharing them.

**NorthSide TasteHub** is simply a way to capture all of that energy and put it somewhere
useful — not just in the comments section.

---

## What NorthSide TasteHub is *not*

Just as important as what TasteHub is, is what it isn’t.

NorthSide TasteHub is **not**:
- a paid ranking system,  
- a sponsored list where money decides who wins,  
- written by a big website based somewhere else,  
- a replacement for your own personal favourites.

Rankings are shaped by **real votes from real people** in and around the towns we serve.
If the community loves a place, it will show.

## Why this matters if you’re thinking about moving north of Toronto

We didn’t create TasteHub as a sales pitch. We created it because **food is one of the
clearest ways to understand what life in a town actually feels like.**

If you’re already living in one of our towns, TasteHub is a fun way to:
- discover new spots,  
- support local restaurants,  
- and settle a few debates along the way.

If you’re thinking about moving from downtown or another suburb to places like
Uxbridge, Georgina, East Gwillimbury, Newmarket, Aurora, Stouffville or Scugog, TasteHub
gives you something most real estate websites don’t:

- a real look at each town’s **food culture**,  
- places the locals actually love,  
- and a small window into what your everyday life here might feel like.

When we walk people through homes in these towns, we’re not just talking about square
footage and lot sizes. We’re pointing out:
- the pizza place that keeps showing up at the top of the poll,  
- the café half the town seems to visit every weekend,  
- or the restaurant that suddenly jumped in votes after people “discovered” it.

It’s one thing to move to a place that looks good on paper.  
It’s another to move somewhere you can already picture your Friday nights.

## How locals can get involved

TasteHub only works if locals are part of it. Here’s how you can plug in:

### If you live in one of our towns

- **Vote** in the polls for your town and favourite categories.  
- **Share** the TasteHub link in your local Facebook group when someone asks where to eat.  
- **Suggest** hidden gems we should add in future polls.  
- **Use it** as a starting point when you want to try somewhere new.

### If you own or manage a restaurant

- Check that we’ve got your **name and location right** in the polls.  
- Share your poll with regulars and invite them to vote.  
- Reach out if you clearly belong in a category but aren’t listed yet.  
- Celebrate your placement — especially if locals push you near the top.

We’re not charging restaurants to be included in polls, and we’re not selling rankings.
If your community loves you, it will show over time.
