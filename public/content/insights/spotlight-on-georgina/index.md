## **Meet the Towns: Spotlight on Georgina**

When Highway 404 ends, Georgina begins. Sitting at the very top of the GTA, Georgina is where the city gives way to open space, lake views, and a more relaxed pace of life.

Stretching along the southern shores of Lake Simcoe, Georgina is one of the NorthSide GTA’s most distinct communities. It’s a place where families, professionals, and long-time locals all share a love for the outdoors and a strong sense of connection. From the beaches of Keswick to the trails of Sutton and the heritage charm of Jackson’s Point, every part of Georgina feels tied to the water and grounded in community.

### **Lakeside Living at Its Best**

Life in Georgina naturally revolves around **Lake Simcoe**. Summers bring boating, fishing, and long days at **Sibbald Point Provincial Park** or **Willow Beach**, while winters transform the shoreline into a playground for ice fishing, skating, and snowmobiling.

It’s a town that embraces all four seasons and makes the most of them. Whether it’s an early morning coffee by the water or a sunset walk along the docks, Georgina offers a sense of balance that’s rare in the GTA.

### **A Growing Community with Character**

Even as it grows, Georgina has never lost its small-town feel. Local markets, family-owned restaurants, and community events make it a welcoming place to live year-round.

Each area has its own personality. **Keswick** is vibrant and convenient, **Sutton** has historic charm and close-knit neighbourhoods, and **Jackson’s Point** blends lakeside leisure with a lively arts and dining scene. Together, they form a community that’s both grounded and growing.

### **Why Buyers Are Taking Notice**

Georgina has quickly become one of the most talked-about towns in the NorthSide GTA. Buyers are drawn to its mix of affordability, space, and lifestyle. You’ll find larger lots, quieter streets, and lakeside homes that offer exceptional value compared to many other GTA communities.

With **Highway 404** now extending directly into town, commuting to Newmarket, Aurora, or even downtown Toronto is easier than ever. Georgina truly offers the best of both worlds — cottage country living with city convenience.

### **The Bottom Line**

Georgina is where the NorthSide truly begins. It’s a place defined by lakefront living, small-town character, and easy access to everything you need.
