## **Meet the Towns: Spotlight on Scugog**

When most people think of lake towns north of Toronto, they picture cottage country — but **Scugog** offers that lifestyle every day. Centered around **Port Perry**, this town brings together lakeside living, small-town charm, and a strong sense of community that makes it one of our favourite places on the NorthSide GTA map.

For anyone looking for a peaceful place to call home while staying within reach of the city, Scugog might surprise you. It’s just far enough to feel calm and quiet, but close enough to keep you connected.

### **A True Lakeside Community**

At the heart of Scugog is **Lake Scugog**, which defines life here. On any given weekend, you’ll find people strolling along the waterfront, boating from the marina, or enjoying lunch at one of the restaurants on **Queen Street** in downtown Port Perry.

The town embraces the water — festivals, concerts, and community events fill the calendar through every season. Even in winter, there’s something special about watching the frozen lake dotted with ice fishing huts and snowmobiles.

It’s the kind of place where the pace slows down just enough to appreciate the view.

### **Historic Charm and Everyday Comfort**

Scugog has a character that feels timeless. **Downtown Port Perry** looks like something out of a movie — historic brick buildings, boutique shops, and local cafés that make every visit feel warm and personal.

At the same time, it’s a fully functional, family-friendly community. Great schools, recreation centres, and nearby conservation areas make it ideal for raising a family or settling into a quieter lifestyle without giving anything up.

**Scugog Shores Heritage Centre and Archives** and the **Town Hall Theatre** keep arts and culture at the forefront, while nearby parks and trails offer endless ways to enjoy the outdoors.

### **Why Buyers Love It**

Scugog has become one of the most appealing towns for buyers looking for space, value, and a stronger sense of community. Homes range from lakeside cottages and historic properties near downtown Port Perry to larger family homes and rural estates surrounded by open countryside.

With **Highway 407** nearby and easy routes to the 401 and 404, commuting across Durham Region or toward Toronto is more convenient than people expect.

### **Our Perspective**

We’ve always seen Scugog as one of those towns that captures the best parts of NorthSide living — a little slower, a little quieter, but full of life and connection.

It’s a place that attracts people who want to enjoy every season, from summer days on the lake to cozy winter nights in a town that still feels close-knit and proud of its roots.

### **The Bottom Line**

Scugog is everything people imagine when they think of living north of the city — but with more warmth, character, and community than you’d ever expect.
