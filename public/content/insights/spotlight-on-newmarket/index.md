## **Meet the Towns: Spotlight on Newmarket**

If there’s one town that truly bridges the gap between city convenience and small-town charm, it’s **Newmarket**. Sitting right in the heart of York Region, Newmarket has become one of the most desirable places to live north of Toronto — and for good reason.

For us, it’s always felt like the perfect balance. You can enjoy tree-lined neighbourhoods, community events, and a walkable downtown while still being just minutes from Highway 404 and the GO Station.

### **A True Community Hub**

Newmarket has grown a lot over the years, but it’s never lost its sense of community. At the centre of it all is **Main Street**, a charming stretch filled with cafés, shops, and restaurants that make it one of our favourite downtowns in the NorthSide GTA.

In the summer, **Riverwalk Commons** becomes a gathering spot for concerts, markets, and festivals. In the winter, the same space turns into an outdoor skating rink surrounded by lights and music. It’s the kind of place where you always bump into someone you know.

### **Lifestyle and Connection**

Newmarket offers the best of both worlds — great schools, parks, and trails for families, along with the convenience of big-city shopping and amenities. The **Magna Centre** is a major highlight, with pools, arenas, and fitness facilities all under one roof.

For commuters, **Highway 404** and the **Newmarket GO Station** make travel simple, and everything from local coffee shops to golf courses is just a short drive away. It’s no surprise that so many GTA families have decided to make the move here.

### **Why Buyers Love It**

Newmarket attracts buyers who want a vibrant, established town that still feels friendly and personal. There’s a wide mix of home styles — from historic properties near Main Street to newer homes in the Stonehaven and Woodland Hill areas.

The town also has a strong focus on parks and green space. Trails connect through the heart of town, making it easy to walk, bike, or explore with family. It’s one of those communities that feels alive year-round.

### **Our Perspective**

As brothers who grew up playing sports across the NorthSide GTA, we’ve always had a soft spot for Newmarket. It’s a town that brings people together — from its local hockey rinks to its farmer’s market and outdoor festivals.

We’ve helped many clients find homes here, and it’s always rewarding to see how quickly they fall in love with the area. Newmarket just feels like home.

### **The Bottom Line**

Newmarket is a perfect example of what life north of the city can be — connected, active, and full of community pride. Whether you’re looking for a first home or your next chapter, it’s a place that offers something for everyone.
