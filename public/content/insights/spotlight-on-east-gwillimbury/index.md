## **Meet the Towns: Spotlight on East Gwillimbury**

Just north of Newmarket sits a community that perfectly captures what life on the NorthSide is all about. **East Gwillimbury** is growing fast, but it still feels relaxed, welcoming, and full of local character.

For many people moving north, it’s the first place that truly feels like a breath of fresh air. With its mix of small-town neighbourhoods, open green spaces, and quick connections to Highway 404, East Gwillimbury offers an incredible balance of convenience and calm.

### **A Town on the Rise**

East Gwillimbury is one of the fastest-growing towns in the NorthSide GTA, but what makes it special is how it’s growing. New neighbourhoods are being built around existing communities, creating a blend of modern living and small-town familiarity.

You’ll find everything from newly built family homes to country properties surrounded by nature. **Sharon**, **Holland Landing**, **Queensville**, and **Mount Albert** each have their own personality — and together, they make East Gwillimbury a town that feels both new and established.

With **Highway 404** running right through it and a short drive to the **East Gwillimbury GO Station**, getting around is simple. You can be in downtown Toronto in under an hour while enjoying quiet evenings surrounded by fields and forests.

### **Where Community Comes First**

What stands out most about East Gwillimbury is its sense of community. There’s a genuine small-town spirit that shows up in everything from local markets to family-run businesses and seasonal festivals.

We’ve always admired how the town has managed to keep that neighbourly feel even as it grows. The **Sharon Temple National Historic Site** is a great example of how East Gwillimbury continues to celebrate its history, while parks, trails, and community centres make it easy for families to connect and get involved.

### **Why Buyers Love It**

East Gwillimbury offers the kind of value that’s getting harder to find closer to the city. Buyers love the space, the newer homes, and the easy access to nature — without losing the benefits of being close to major highways, shopping, and amenities in Newmarket and Aurora.

It’s an especially great choice for families and first-time buyers who want to grow into a community, not just move to a neighbourhood.

### **Our Perspective**

We’ve always felt that East Gwillimbury represents what the NorthSide GTA stands for — more community, more value, and more room to live the way you want. It’s a town that’s growing in all the right ways, and it’s exciting to see how many families are discovering it for the first time.

### **The Bottom Line**

East Gwillimbury is a place where growth meets quality of life. It’s modern, family-friendly, and surrounded by nature, while still being close to everything you need.
