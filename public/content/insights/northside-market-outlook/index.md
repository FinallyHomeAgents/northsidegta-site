## Inventory is finally catching up

The NorthSide GTA is seeing one of its strongest new listing waves in two years, with active inventory up 18% month over month. That means buyers exploring Aurora, Uxbridge, and Georgina are stepping into **far more choice** than they had last fall.

![Sunrise over Lake Simcoe](/uploads/insights/inline-market.png "Lake Simcoe mornings are still the best motivator to head north.")

### What this means for buyers

- More detached homes entering the market above $1.1M
- Growing selection of renovated bungalows near Lake Simcoe
- Stabilizing days-on-market just under three weeks

> "When we map showings, we’re seeing northbound interest picking up every weekend."

### How sellers can win right now

Pricing remains critical. Homes aligned with **hyper-local comps** are selling with strong engagement—especially when marketing highlights commute times, lifestyle perks, and smart-home upgrades. Staging with natural textures, layered lighting, and biophilic touches continues to resonate with Toronto move-up buyers.
