PRESS RELEASE: Georgeina adds an “E” after George goes deep

GEORGEINA, ON — Oct 21, 2025

After a night of pure Blue Jays magic, the Town of Georgina has officially added an “E,” becoming Georgeina — in honour of George Springer’s go-ahead home run in the 7th inning of Game 7 of the ALCS.

The emergency council vote to add the extra letter passed unanimously, 9–0.

“George gave us all something to cheer about, so we figured the least we could do was give him an ‘E’,” said the Mayor with a smile. “This town bleeds blue, and this little change celebrates the pride and excitement uniting our community.”
