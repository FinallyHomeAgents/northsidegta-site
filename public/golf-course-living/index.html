Uxbridge / A closer look

At Wyndance, golf can be part of the home purchase.

A distinctive course. A nearby estate-home community. A different conversation about what comes with the keys.

Wyndance is Greg Norman’s first Canadian course, built around a former quarry, with an 18-hole layout and the nine-hole Down Under Academy course. For buyers considering Uxbridge, the adjoining Wyndance Estates community adds a real estate angle worth investigating.

Homes in Wyndance Estates have been marketed with a deeded ClubLink membership included. That is different from having your annual golf costs paid: active golf membership carries annual dues, food-and-beverage minimums and other applicable charges. Confirm the membership entitlement, activation or transfer requirements, and current costs for the specific home with ClubLink and the seller’s documents.

Where we can help: compare suitable Wyndance Estates homes with other Uxbridge options, and identify the property and club questions to resolve before you make a decision.

Membership detail and sources

ClubLink’s 2023 homeowner information sheet lists active, social, clubhouse and dormant statuses. It is historical evidence of the arrangement, not a current fee quote. We have deliberately omitted its old prices. Membership inclusion must be verified property by property.

Common questions

Good questions before a move.

Which towns offer public golf?

Uxbridge, Aurora and Stouffville all have public-access options. Confirm current booking terms with the operators.

Do I need a membership?

No. Several highlighted courses are publicly bookable, while private clubs and membership networks follow different policies.

Does buying nearby include membership?

Some Wyndance Estates homes have been marketed with a deeded ClubLink membership. Confirm the entitlement for the specific property, plus current annual dues, activation or transfer terms and other charges. Living near a course elsewhere does not automatically include membership.

What matters besides course distance?

Work commutes, neighbourhood services, property maintenance, costs and actual address-to-course routes.

Do I have to buy directly on a golf course?

No. We can compare homes beside a course with homes a drive away. Tell us whether a view, club community or convenient golf access matters most. A course-side address does not guarantee access to the club.

What types of homes could we compare?

Your search could include detached homes, bungalows, townhomes or rural properties, depending on current availability and your preferences. We will distinguish homes actually on a course from those nearby, and review ownership and maintenance details.

What budget do I need for a home near golf?

There is no reliable single price for all three towns. Home type, lot, condition and location affect the comparison. Share a comfortable range and we can use current listings and recent comparable sales to help assess the options. This guide does not advertise a starting price or imply that specific homes are available.

What will my golf-and-home shortlist include?

A focused selection of suitable available homes, neighbourhood context, relevant golf options and membership questions to investigate. Tell us your budget, preferred town and must-haves so the response can be specific to you.

Can you help if I need to sell before buying?

Yes. Tell us about your current home and your intended move. We can discuss the sale and purchase together, including timing and the information needed to plan the next step.

Are these the only golf courses in the area?

No. This is a curated selection of 13 courses in and around Uxbridge, Aurora and Stouffville, not an exhaustive directory or an objective ranking. Confirm current access and membership terms directly with the clubs.
